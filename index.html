Common Questions

Everything You Want to Know

What exactly is hydrogen water and how is it different?
Hydrogen water is ultra-purified water infused with dissolved molecular hydrogen (H₂) gas. Unlike regular water, it contains additional H₂ molecules that act as selective antioxidants — small enough to penetrate every cell in your body. They target and neutralize only the harmful free radicals while leaving your body's beneficial reactive oxygen species intact.
Why add electrolytes to hydrogen water?
Electrolytes like sodium, potassium, and magnesium help your body absorb and retain water more efficiently. While hydrogen works at the cellular level to reduce oxidative stress and inflammation, electrolytes restore your body's natural fluid balance and keep muscles and nerves functioning properly. Together, they deliver complete hydration that plain water simply cannot.
Is hydrogen water backed by real science?
Yes — extensively. Over 1,000 peer-reviewed studies in journals like PubMed and PMC document molecular hydrogen's potential to reduce inflammation, fight oxidative stress, support muscle recovery, improve exercise performance, and enhance cognitive function. This isn't wellness marketing — it's published clinical science.
How many calories are in Elevated ICON?
Zero. Every single flavor — Pure Water, Watermelon, Lemon & Lime, and Strawberry Kiwi — contains 0 calories, 0 sugar, and 0 fat. The flavored variants add only natural flavors to the same clean base. Clean label always.
When is the best time to drink it?
Anytime is the right time. For athletes, before and after workouts maximizes recovery benefits. For focus, first thing in the morning supports mental clarity without caffeine. The truth is — it's simply better water, and every sip is a good one.
